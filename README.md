# Airline Operations Financial Impact & Operational Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project investigating flight delays, airport gridlocks, and their downstream financial impacts. This project translates raw operational aviation records into actionable business insights using SQL for data aggregation and Excel for executive-level dashboard reporting.

## 📊 Business Intelligence Dashboards
* **Executive Summary:** High-level operational overview showcasing overall failure rates.
* **Financial Impact & Operational Risk Analysis:** Slicing the $6.20B total loss across airlines, airports, and time trends.
* **Airport Performance Summary:** Deep-dive into local airport bottlenecks, cancellation anomalies, and runway congestion.
* **Airline Performance Analytics:** Structural operational review tracking carrier schedule vulnerabilities and cascading downstream failures.

## 📈 Key Insights & Business Impact
* **Total Operational Loss:** Delayed flights created a massive **$6.20 Billion** operational loss across the network based on a $75/minute operational delay cost model.
* **Primary Cost Drivers:** Southwest Airlines (**WN at $871M**) and American Airlines (**AA at $676M**) represent the largest corporate financial leaks by carrier. 
* **Hub Infrastructure Vulnerabilities:** Chicago O'Hare (**ORD at $484M**) and Atlanta (**ATL at $405M**) stand out as the critical epicenters for tarmac gridlock and severe multi-hour delays.
* **Network Risks:** Tracking incident counts shows that WN suffers from a high volume of cascading schedule failures, proving that tightly packed carrier schedules lack the operational buffers to absorb initial morning delays.
* **Seasonal Vulnerability Window:** Financial losses and operational failure rates spike aggressively during peak mid-summer (June/July) and winter travel surges.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methodology
* **Database Management:** SQL Server / T-SQL (Conditional Aggregations, Subqueries, Type Conversions, and Missing Value Handling)
* **Business Intelligence:** Advanced Excel (Dynamic Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Dual-Axis Combo Charts, Custom KPI Metrics, and Layout Design)
