# ✈️ Airline Operations Financial Impact & Operational Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project investigating airline flight delays, airport operational bottlenecks, and their estimated financial impact.

The project transforms raw airline operational data into actionable business insights using **SQL, Excel, and Power BI**.

The analysis focuses on identifying high-risk airlines and airports, understanding the major causes of delays, analyzing performance trends, and estimating the financial impact associated with operational disruptions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Flight delays and cancellations can create significant operational and financial consequences for airlines and airports.

Management needs to understand:

* Which airlines experience the highest delay rates?
* Which airports have the greatest operational bottlenecks?
* What are the primary causes of flight delays?
* How does delay performance change over time?
* Which airlines and airports have the greatest estimated financial impact?
* Is there a relationship between severe delays and tarmac congestion?
* Where should management prioritize operational improvements?

This project uses data analysis to answer these questions and support evidence-based decision-making.

## 🔍 Key Findings

Based on the current analysis:

* The network recorded approximately **1.94 million flights**.
* Approximately **1.22 million flights were delayed**.
* The overall flight delay rate was approximately **62.73%**.
* The cancellation rate was approximately **0.70%**.
* The estimated financial impact was approximately **$6.20 billion**.
* Significant differences exist in delay performance across airlines and airports.
* Late-aircraft, carrier, and NAS-related disruptions represent major contributors to total delay duration.
* Certain airports demonstrate substantially higher delay rates than the overall network.

> **Note:** Financial impact figures are estimates derived from the dataset and should not be interpreted as reported financial statements of actual airlines.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, airlines and airport operators could:

### 1. Prioritize high-risk airports

Focus operational improvement initiatives on airports with consistently high delay rates and significant financial exposure.

### 2. Address late-aircraft disruptions

Investigate turnaround times, aircraft scheduling, and operational dependencies contributing to late-aircraft delays.

### 3. Monitor airline-specific risk

Airlines performing significantly above the network-average delay rate should receive additional operational review.

### 4. Monitor congestion-related disruptions

Investigate the relationship between tarmac congestion and severe delays to identify potential capacity and scheduling improvements.

### 5. Use financial impact to prioritize resources

Operational improvements should prioritize disruptions with the greatest estimated financial consequences rather than focusing only on the number of delayed flights.
